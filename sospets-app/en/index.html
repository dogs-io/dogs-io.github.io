Our story

The S.O.S Pets organization was established in 1978 by Riki Bassry, the late Dr. Ze’ev Sheiner, and the late Mona Halfman, with the aim of helping homeless dogs and cats and improving the condition of animals in Israel. Riki Bassry, who still serves as the volunteer chairman of the association, is the living spirit behind the association’s activities throughout the years.

S.O.S Pets opposes animal euthanasia and has adhered to these ethics since the day it was founded. The organization fights for every dog or cat’s life and quality of life. The S.O.S Pets association was the first in Israel to operate—and still operates—without shelters, based on the principle and belief that human beings should take care of their animals, not abandon them, and take responsibility for their fate.

We believe

As part of a holistic approach, we believe that cats and dogs play a vital role in maintaining a sensitive and caring community. Cats and dogs are part of our lives, and it is our obligation to prevent their suffering, offer them a decent quality of life, and provide proper care.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Do I need an account to view animals and adoption information?

No. You can browse animals and adoption information without signing in. Sign-in is required only for protected volunteer and staff tools.

Does the app replace the SOS Pets website?

No. The organization website continues to present SOS Pets activities. The app adds a convenient mobile experience that connects animal discovery, adoption preparation, and adoption-event information.

Is the animal information current?

Public information comes from SOS Pets sources and is updated in near real time. If a detail is important to your decision, confirm it with the team at the adoption event.

Is the app available for Android and iPhone?

Yes. Download it from Google Play or the Apple App Store using the buttons on this page.

For approved volunteers and staff

The same app. More tools for each role.

After sign-in and role approval, the app adds purpose-built tools for daily work. Each person sees only the information needed for their role, and phones never connect directly to the organization’s core systems.
